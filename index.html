あ

JLPT N5 Beginner Course

Start learning Japanese from the basics and build the foundation needed for the JLPT N5 examination.

~100Kanji
~800Words
80/180Pass mark

What You Will Learn

  • Reading and writing Hiragana and Katakana
  • Basic Kanji (about 100 characters)
  • Core vocabulary: numbers, dates, time, family, food
  • Basic grammar: particles, です/ます, adjectives, て-form
  • Self-introduction, shopping, directions and daily life
  • Short reading passages and slow, clear listening
Ask About JLPT N5 →
日本

JLPT N4 Elementary Course

Build on your basic Japanese knowledge and develop skills for understanding everyday Japanese.

~300Kanji
~1,500Words
90/180Pass mark

What You Will Learn

  • Plain form, た-form, ない-form and verb groups
  • Potential, passive, causative and conditional (〜たら・〜ば)
  • Basic polite and respectful expressions (keigo)
  • Kanji up to about 300 characters
  • Vocabulary up to about 1,500 words
  • Everyday conversation and longer reading and listening practice
